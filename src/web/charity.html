Charity Check

Before you give, check that the charity is registered. Search by name or by its EIN to see its tax-exempt status, when it was recognised, and its latest filings with revenue, expenses and assets.

Covers US-registered nonprofits only. Charities registered in other countries are not in this data.

What it checks

  • Registration: the organisation's IRS record, its tax-exempt subsection (501(c)(3) is a charity that can receive tax-deductible gifts), the year the exemption was granted and its NTEE category.
  • Filings: the latest Form 990 filings with revenue, expenses and assets as the IRS extracts report them, with links to the filing PDFs. The share spent on programmes is shown only when the filing data has it.
  • Warnings: no filings in the last three years, a revocation when the record shows one, an organisation that is not a 501(c)(3), and search results with a name very close to a well-known charity but a different EIN.

A registered charity is not automatically a good one, and a scammer can quote a real charity's name. Give through the charity's own website, and match the EIN on the receipt. Data: ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, from IRS records. JSON at /api/charity?q=.
