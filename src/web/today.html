How the wire is picked

  • One per desk first. The strongest signal of each desk goes in before any desk gets a second line, so a busy night of aircraft cannot fill the wire.
  • Six at most. A desk holds two lines at most. Duplicate titles are dropped.
  • Only what was stored. Watchlist, cases, geofences, social surges and the checks people run are never on the wire.

Scores are Oculon's 0-100 signal strength, not a measure of harm. JSON at /api/wire, image at /wire/today.png.
