Security Checkup

Five minutes. One grade.

Check the password you use most, how well your email domain is protected, and six habits that stop most account takeovers. You get a score from 0 to 100, a grade, and the fixes that matter most.

1 · A password you use (optional)

It is hashed in this browser. Only the first 5 characters of the hash are sent, so nobody, including Oculon, sees the password.

2 · Your email (optional)

Only the part after the @ is sent, to check whether mail from that domain can be forged and whether the domain has had breaches.

3 · A crypto wallet (optional)

A public address only. It is read for risky token approvals, the same check as Wallet Guard.

4 · Six habits

Do you use a password manager?
How do you sign in to your email with two-step sign-in?
Does every account have its own password?
Is there a SIM PIN or a port-out lock with your carrier?
How do your phone, computer and browser update?
Are your files and photos backed up?

5 · Your result

What is sent

  • Password: never. The browser sends 5 hex characters of its SHA-1 hash to /api/pwned and compares the rest itself (Have I Been Pwned's range method).
  • Email: only the domain, to /api/emailsec and /api/breaches.
  • Score: your answers and the counts above go to /api/checkup, which scores them and keeps nothing. The share card shows only the grade and score.

A score reads your answers at one moment and cannot see everything; it is guidance, not a guarantee. Breach data from Have I Been Pwned (CC BY 4.0).
