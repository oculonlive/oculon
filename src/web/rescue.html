Good to know

  • No one can get the money back for a fee. "Recovery" services that contact you after a scam are a second scam.
  • Banks, police and exchanges never ask for your password, a one-time code or your recovery phrase.
  • Reporting helps even when the money is gone: it can stop the same scam reaching the next person.
