Paste terms of service, a privacy policy, a subscription, rental or job contract, or give its link. Oculon points out the clauses worth reading twice and quotes each one exactly as written.

What it looks for

  • Money: automatic renewal, cancellation fees, no refunds, early termination penalties and liquidated damages.
  • Your rights: forced arbitration, class action and jury trial waivers, liability and warranty waivers, courts in a far-away place.
  • Your data: selling or sharing it with advertisers, location, biometric and contacts collection, data kept after you delete it.
  • Control: broad licences to what you upload, terms that change without your consent, accounts closed without notice, non-compete clauses.

Clauses are found by fixed rules in code and every finding quotes the sentence it matched. When an AI model is available it adds a short summary that may only refer to those quotes. This is not legal advice, and local law may make some clauses unenforceable.
