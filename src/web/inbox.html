Forward as an attachment for the full check

A normal forward copies the text but drops the original headers, which say whether the sender's domain really sent it (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) and which servers it passed through. Forwarded as an attachment, the email arrives untouched and everything can be checked.

  • Gmail (web): open the email, choose the three-dot menu, then Forward as attachment.
  • Outlook: open the email, choose More actions (three dots), then Forward as attachment.
  • Apple Mail: select the email, then Message > Forward as Attachment.
  • Phone apps often cannot attach an email. A normal forward still works: the text, links and attachments are checked, and the reply says that the headers were not available.

Do not click anything in the suspicious email before forwarding it. Forwarding is safe; the links inside are only opened by the check services, never in your browser.

What is kept

The raw emailNot stored. It is read in memory for the check and then dropped.
The check resultKept for 90 days, like every Oculon check: the verdict, the reasons, the subject and sender of the checked email, attachment names and fingerprints (SHA-256). It is shown only at the report link in your reply, which uses a random id.
Your addressKept only as a one-way hash with the date and the verdict, for the daily limits, and removed after 30 days. It is used for nothing else and never added to a mailing list.
Who sends the replyThe reply is sent through Resend, an email delivery service, to the address you forwarded from.

Limits

  • Up to 10 checks per sender per day, and 300 per day for the whole inbox. Over the limit, no reply is sent.
  • Emails up to 5 MB, including attachments.
  • No reply to automatic messages (out-of-office replies, mailing lists, bounces), or when the sending domain failed authentication, so the inbox cannot be used to send mail to someone else.
  • Free for everyone. No account needed.

A verdict is what the sources found at that moment, not a guarantee that an email is safe. Oculon never asks for passwords, codes or payments by email. Prefer a page? Upload the .eml file in the app instead.
