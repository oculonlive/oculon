Is It Down

Is it down, or is it just you? Popular services, read from their official status pages. For big services that publish no status feed, Oculon makes one small request from its own server, and that result is labelled as our probe.

Where the answers come from

  • Official status page: the company's own Atlassian Statuspage (or Slack's status API). It shows the overall state, components with problems and open incidents with their latest update. It only shows what the company has chosen to report.
  • Our probe from one location: for services such as X, Google, YouTube, Instagram, WhatsApp and Telegram, which publish no public status feed, the Oculon server makes one small HTTPS request and records the answer and how long it took. Any answer means the service is reachable from our server. No answer can also be a problem on our side or in one region, so it is never an official outage report.
  • Answers are cached for two and a half minutes.

JSON at /api/down and /api/down?service=github.
