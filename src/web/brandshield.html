Brand Shield

Who is pretending to be your brand right now? Give your domain and Oculon looks for lookalike domains that exist, new certificates naming the brand, which of them are live, and whether the most suspicious ones are already flagged. Each result comes with its reasons.

A scan can take up to two minutes: the certificate logs are often slow.

How it ranks a domain

  • Name: it contains the brand, or imitates it with a typo, a swapped letter or a lookalike character. Words such as login, secure, verify, support, wallet, airdrop or claim add weight.
  • Where: endings often used for abuse (.xyz, .top, .click and others) and punycode add weight.
  • When: a certificate issued in the last 7 or 30 days, found in Certificate Transparency logs, means a site is being set up.
  • Live: the domain resolves in DNS, and mail servers mean it can send email in the brand's name.
  • Flagged: the top live ones go through the Oculon URL check (Safe Browsing, URLhaus and phishing blocklists, when available, plus domain age).

A score ranks leads to investigate. It is not proof of phishing: some domains are parked, owned by the brand, or belong to unrelated businesses. Data: crt.sh and CertSpotter, Google DNS, and the sources of the URL check. JSON at /api/brandshield?domain=.
