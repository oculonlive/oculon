Discord Sentinel

Scam links out.
Impersonators flagged.

A bot for your Discord server. Every link posted is checked; malicious ones are removed with a short notice, suspicious ones get a warning. Members who copy the name of your owner, admins or moderators are flagged to your team before they start sending DMs.

Links

Each link goes through the Oculon URL check: Google Safe Browsing, URLhaus, phishing and wallet-drainer blocklists, domain age and look-alike signs. Results are cached per link.

Impersonation

On join and on every name change, the display name is compared with your staff after folding look-alike letters: 0 and o, 1, l and i, rn and m, Cyrillic and Greek copies, spaces and symbols.

Commands

/check a link or a pasted message, privately. /sentinel settings sets the log channel and turns auto-delete on or off.

What it does in your server

Malicious linkThe message is deleted when auto-delete is on and the bot has Manage Messages in that channel, and a notice names the sender, the domain (written so it cannot be clicked) and the reasons. Without the permission, or with auto-delete off, it replies with a warning instead. The log channel gets a copy.
Suspicious linkA reply warns about the link and says why. Nothing is deleted.
Look-alike nameA notice in the log channel, or the server's system channel when none is set: who joined or renamed, and which staff member the name copies. The bot does not kick or ban; your team decides.
Who counts as staffThe server owner and members whose roles have Administrator, Manage Server, Manage Roles, Manage Messages, Kick, Ban or Timeout permissions.

Links to large platforms (Discord itself, YouTube, GitHub, Tenor, Giphy and similar) are not checked each time. Messages from bots and webhooks are skipped. Each server can check up to 120 new links an hour; links checked before are answered from the cache.

Permissions it asks for

  • View Channels and Read Message History: to see the messages it checks.
  • Send Messages: for notices and warnings. Command answers need no extra permission.
  • Manage Messages: only to delete messages with malicious links. Leave it out and the bot warns instead.
  • Message content and server members access: to read the links in messages and to see joins and name changes.

Only people with Manage Server can change /sentinel settings. The bot never pings anyone in its notices.

What is kept

Per server: the log channel and the auto-delete setting. Message text is read to find links and is not stored. Link verdicts are cached in memory for six hours. A /check is stored like every Oculon check, for 90 days, at a report link only you get.

Price

Free for every server. Holders of the Oculon token may get extra servers or higher link limits later; nothing about that is set yet.

A link verdict is what the sources found at that moment. "No known threats" is not a promise that a page is safe.
