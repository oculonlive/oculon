How it reads a launch

  • Every new pool on Uniswap v2, v3 and v4 and on Pons gets a quick read from its pool alone: thin liquidity, buys with no sells (the honeypot shape), a crash in the last hour, a valuation far above its liquidity.
  • Tokens that draw liquidity get the full check: whether the contract source is verified, how much the 10 largest holders own, every pool, and how many of the deployer's earlier tokens are dead.
  • For two days the liquidity is read again. A pool that held at least $3,000 and lost more than 80% of it is marked RUGGED. A launch that never drew liquidity and is empty after a day is marked DEAD.

A verdict reads public data at one moment. It is not proof of intent, and a clean read today does not promise tomorrow. Nothing here is financial advice. Data: GeckoTerminal and the Robinhood Chain Blockscout explorer. JSON at /api/rh/launches.
