Wallet Guard

Who can still
move your tokens?

Every approval your wallet ever signed, checked against what the chain still allows today. Risky spenders are flagged, and you can revoke them from your own wallet in one click. Nothing is custodied; Oculon never asks you to sign anything except the revoke you choose.

Scanning works without connecting.

On-chain features are paused on this install.

What it checks

  • Every Approval and ApprovalForAll your wallet emitted, from the chain's explorer, then the allowance each spender still has today, read live from the chain. Used up and revoked approvals drop out.
  • Who the spender is: a known router or marketplace, a verified contract, an unverified contract, or a plain wallet. Unlimited access for an unverified contract or a wallet is how drainers keep access after one bad signature.
  • Fake approvals that spam tokens emit to scare you are filtered out.

How revoke works

A revoke is an ordinary transaction from your wallet to the token: approve(spender, 0), or setApprovalForAll(spender, false) for NFTs. Your wallet shows it before you sign and you pay the gas. Oculon never sees your keys. Check the token and spender in your wallet's prompt before you confirm.

A spender being flagged reads public data, it is not proof it is malicious. Not financial advice.
