Connect your wallet

To use your holding, connect the wallet that holds OCULON and sign the request. Signing sends no transaction and costs nothing. To burn instead:

  1. From your wallet, send the amount of OCULON to the burn address 0x000000000000000000000000000000000000dEaD on Robinhood Chain.
  2. Copy the transaction hash once it is confirmed.
  3. Connect the same wallet below and sign the request. Only the wallet that burned can use its burn, and each burn counts once.
