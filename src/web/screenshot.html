Screenshot Check

Got a strange text, email, DM or ad? Drop a screenshot of it. Oculon reads the words and links in the picture, then checks them the same way it checks a pasted message: known scam patterns, and every link against blocklists and domain records.

Drop a screenshot here or tap to choose, or paste with Ctrl+V · PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 8 MB

How it works

  • Reading: a free AI vision model (Google Gemini) copies the visible text word for word and lists the links, handles, phone numbers, amounts and brand names it sees. It is not asked for an opinion.
  • Judging: the verdict comes from Oculon's scam check in plain code: urgency, fees, prizes, one-time codes, crypto and gift card requests, impersonated brands, and each link through Safe Browsing, URLhaus, urlscan.io and domain age.
  • Links are shown defanged (hxxps://example[.]com) so nothing on this page can be clicked by accident.

The image is sent to Google's Gemini API to read it and is not stored by Oculon beyond the check record. Do not upload screenshots with passwords, codes or card numbers. A clean result is not proof a message is safe.
