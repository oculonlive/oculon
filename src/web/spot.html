How to read a link

  • Read the domain from the right. In connect.secure.wellsfargo.com the domain is wellsfargo.com; everything before it is a subdomain the owner controls.
  • A brand name anywhere else proves nothing. metamask-claim.example belongs to whoever owns example.
  • Free site builders such as pages.dev or vercel.app host real projects too, but a wallet or bank will not ask you to sign in there.
  • The padlock and https only mean the connection is encrypted. Most phishing sites have them.

Scam links are defanged (hxxps, [.]) and never clickable: do not type them into a browser. Scams come from the ScamSniffer blocklist; legitimate pages come from a fixed list of official addresses. JSON at /api/spot.
