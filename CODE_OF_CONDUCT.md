# Code of conduct

Oculon is a tool for understanding what public sources say. The project asks the same care of the people who build it.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Keep discussion on the work: code, data, documentation, design.
- Credit others and their sources.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or personal attacks.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including information found with Oculon.
- Using project spaces to target, stalk or dox private individuals.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who do not follow this code. Report problems privately through the repository's **Security** tab (choose **Report a vulnerability** and say it is a conduct report) or to a maintainer.
